# Privileged access at Google Cloud Dedicated in Germany



Google Cloud Dedicated in Germany systems are built with a focus on protecting your content
using controls and monitoring. Your content stored on Google Cloud Dedicated in Germany
completely belongs to you. Occasionally, personnel might need to access
your content but these accesses are never without a valid business
justification.

## Why personnel request access to Customer Data

The most common reason why personnel request access to
[Customer Data](https://berlin.devsitetest.how/terms/service-terms) is to resolve a
customer support ticket. If you create a customer support request,
then a personnel might be required to request access to your
data. Access Transparency exists to provide customers visibility into these accesses.

## What is privileged access

personnel's access to your data to fulfill an obligation of
providing a contracted service is called *privileged access*. Access to your
data in Google Cloud Dedicated in Germany is usually because of the following reasons:

- You are accessing your own data.

- A service you are using is accessing data on your behalf.

When requested to provide a contracted service, personnel
acting as a privileged administrator can access your data.

## Foundational principles of privileged access management

Google Cloud Dedicated in Germany's privileged access management strategy strictly limits what a
single staff member can view and do with your data.
Google Cloud Dedicated in Germany's privileged access philosophy is based on the following principles:

- 

**Least privilege**: Access to Customer Data is denied by default for all
personnel. When access is granted, it is temporary and no greater than
what is absolutely necessary to provide the contracted service.

- 

**Limit singular access to data**: Singularly accessing Customer Data without
another individual involved is extremely difficult for any and every
personnel.

- 

**All access must be justified**: By default, personnel don't
have access to Customer Data. personnel can access your data
only with an **active**, valid business justification. 
personnel can't access Customer Data for justifications that are closed or
where the person is not a directly linked collaborator.
For the list of valid business justifications,
see [Justification reason codes](/assured-workloads/access-transparency/docs/reading-logs#justification-reason-codes).

- 

**Monitor and alerting**: Monitoring and response processes exist to identify,
triage, and remediate violations of these principles.
