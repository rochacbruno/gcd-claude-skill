## Product launch stages

### Preview

At Preview, products or features are ready for testing by customers. Preview
offerings are often publicly announced, but are not necessarily
feature-complete.

### General Availability

General Availability products and features are open to all customers, ready for
production use.

### Deprecated

Deprecated features are scheduled to be shut down and removed.
