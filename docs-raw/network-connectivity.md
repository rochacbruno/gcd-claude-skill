Network Connectivity is Google's suite of products that provide
enterprise connectivity from your on-premises network or from another cloud
provider to your Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) network. NCC also
lets you create VPC spokes to connect VPC networks
together for full mesh connectivity.

Solutions for connecting your networks to Google.

](/network-connectivity/docs/how-to/choose-product)

### Key terms



Key terminology that applies to Network Connectivity products.

](/network-connectivity/docs/concepts/key-terms)









- 
[

### Cloud VPN



Securely extends your peer network to Google's network through an IPsec VPN tunnel.

](/network-connectivity/docs/vpn)

- 
[

### Cloud Interconnect



Extends your on-premises network to Google's network through a highly available,
low latency connection. You can use Dedicated Interconnect to connect
directly to Google, or use Partner Interconnect to connect to Google
through a supported service provider.

](/network-connectivity/docs/interconnect)

- 
[

### Cloud Router



Uses the Border Gateway Protocol (BGP) to dynamically exchange routes between
your VPC network and peer networks when you use
Cloud VPN or Cloud Interconnect.

](/network-connectivity/docs/router)

- 
[

### Network Connectivity Center



Network Connectivity Center lets you use a hub-and-spoke architecture for network connectivity
management. With this architecture, you can conduct data
transfer between your sites. NCC lets you create
VPC spokes to connect VPC networks together
for full mesh connectivity. It also
includes the Router appliance feature, which lets you use a third-party
network virtual appliance to establish site-to-site or
site-to-cloud connectivity.

](/network-connectivity/docs/network-connectivity-center)









- 
[

### Verified Peering Provider



Lets you reach all publicly available Google Cloud Dedicated in Germany resources through an internet
service provider, without the need to directly peer with Google.

](/network-connectivity/docs/verified-peering-provider)

- 
[

### Direct Peering



Enables you to establish a direct peering connection between your business
network and Google's edge network and exchange high-throughput cloud traffic.

](/network-connectivity/docs/direct-peering)

- 
[

### Carrier Peering



Enables you to peer with Google by connecting through
a support provider that in turn peers with Google.

](/network-connectivity/docs/carrier-peering)









- 
[

### CDN Interconnect



Enables select CDN providers to establish
direct peering links with Google's edge network at various locations, which
enables you to direct your traffic from your VPC networks
to a provider's network.

](/network-connectivity/docs/cdn-interconnect)
